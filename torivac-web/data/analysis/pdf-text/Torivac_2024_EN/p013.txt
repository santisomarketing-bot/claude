VARIABLE AUTOTRANSFORMERS




Motorised regulation

Voltage variators with motorised regulation have three connection
terminals corresponding to the motorised supplement. To increase the
output voltage on the inverter, 230 VAC voltage must be applied
between the wired terminals Violet - Grey. To decrease the output
voltage at the speed variator, 230 VAC voltage must be applied between
the wired terminals Orange - Grey.

MAINTENANCE

In order to extend the service life of the voltage variator, we recommend the following:
• If possible, place the inverter to prevent the accumulation of dust on the contact track.
• Periodically check the status of the contact carbons and replace them when necessary.
• Periodically, check the status of the contact track so that, if necessary, a complete overhaul of the voltage variator can be carried out at our
facilities.


Protection

• We recommend protect the voltage variator output by inserting a fuse with a value 10% higher than the rated current between terminal 2
and the load.
• If you wish to protect the input, a delayed-action fuse or circuit breaker should be placed between the mains and terminal 1, because of the
high voltage peak that occurs when coils start up.
